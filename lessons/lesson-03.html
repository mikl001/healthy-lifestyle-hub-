📰 Reading

Why do we love junk food? It tastes great. But behind colourful packages are scary facts: lots of sugar, salt, and fat, plus chemicals like additives and preservatives. A burger and fries have ~1000 calories — half of what a teen needs a day! And these are «empty» calories.

The smart alternative: home-cooked meals with fresh vegetables, fruits, whole grains, and lean protein. Organic food has fewer chemicals.

Key word: BALANCE. A burger once a month — fine. Every day — problem!
