📰 Reading

Just 30 minutes of exercise a day can change your life! Three main types:

1. Cardio — running, swimming, cycling. Makes your heart strong, gives stamina.

2. Strength — push-ups, squats, weights. Strong muscles.

3. Flexibility — stretching, yoga. Prevents injuries.

Don't forget warm-up — 5 min before exercise! A fit body = a fit mind.
