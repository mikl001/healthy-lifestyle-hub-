🎤 Useful Phrases for Presentations

Opening: «Good morning, everyone! Today I'd like to talk about…»

Main part: «First of all… Secondly… Moreover…»

Conclusion: «To sum up… In conclusion… My main message is…»

Q&A: «That's a great question. Are there any questions?»

📰 Structure of a 3-minute presentation

  • 0:00–0:30 — Opening (greeting + hook + topic)
  • 0:30–2:30 — Main content (3 key points)
  • 2:30–3:00 — Conclusion + call to action
  • + Q&A

💡 SMILE. Eye contact. Speak SLOWLY.
