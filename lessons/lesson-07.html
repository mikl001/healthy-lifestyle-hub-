📰 Reading

Teenagers need 8–9 hours of sleep every night. Lack of sleep causes: poor memory, bad mood, weak immune system. Insomnia — when you can't fall asleep.

Tips: Get to bed at the same time. No screens 30 min before bedtime. Cool, dark, quiet room. Use an alarm clock, not your phone. Don't oversleep at weekends — it confuses your body!
