📰 Reading: «The Food Pyramid»

The food pyramid is a simple way to show what to eat every day. At the bottom we have grains: bread, rice, pasta. They give us energy. Above we have vegetables and fruits with vitamins and minerals. Then come dairy products (milk, cheese) and protein (fish, meat, beans). Higher up there are oils. At the top — sweets and fast food. Don't forget to drink water — at least 6 glasses every day!
