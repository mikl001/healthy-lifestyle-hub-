📰 Reading: «What Makes a Lifestyle Healthy?»

Everybody talks about a healthy lifestyle, but what does it actually mean? A healthy lifestyle is not just about eating salads or running every day. It is a balance of many things.

First, we need physical activity. Walking, dancing, swimming, or playing sports — anything that gets you off the sofa — is good for your heart and muscles.

Second, we need to eat the right food. Not all food gives us energy. A healthy diet has fruits, vegetables, grains, and protein.

Third, sleep matters a lot. Teenagers need 8–9 hours of sleep every night.

Fourth, mental well-being is just as important as physical health.

Finally, a healthy lifestyle means avoiding bad habits. Prevention is always better than treatment.

Health is something we build every day. Your attitude matters more than you think.

📌 Опорный конспект

🌱 Healthy lifestyle = balance of 5 things:

5. 🚫 Avoiding bad habits
