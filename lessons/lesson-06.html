📰 Reading

Most common illnesses are not serious. Cold: runny nose, sore throat, mild fever. Rest, drink tea, no antibiotics. Flu: high fever, body aches. Must stay in bed. If you feel dizzy or have serious symptoms — call an ambulance. Prevention is best: wash hands, eat well, sleep, exercise!
