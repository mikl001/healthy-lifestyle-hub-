🔠 Imperative Mood

Утверждение: Eat fresh fruits! Drink water!

Отрицание: Don't smoke! Don't skip breakfast!

Усиление: Always wash your hands! Never eat junk food before bed!

📰 How to make a great poster

  1. Choose your topic — one strong message
  2. Write a slogan — short catchy phrase (4–6 words)
  3. Plan the layout — top: title, middle: visuals + tips, bottom: call to action
  4. Add content — bullet points 5–8 words each
  5. Design — 2–3 colours, big readable fonts
  6. Check — can it be understood in 5 seconds?
