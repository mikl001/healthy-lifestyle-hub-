  • If you have a goal, you will feel motivated.

📰 Secrets of Long-Living People

  1. Move naturally — walk, garden, climb. Not gym.
  2. Eat plants — 80% of the plate. Meat — rarely.
  3. Have a purpose («ikigai» in Japan). If you have a goal, you will feel motivated.
  4. Stay social — loneliness is as bad as smoking 15 cigarettes a day!
  5. Manage stress — meditate, pray, relax.

💪 Motivation gets you started, but dedication keeps you going.
