🔠 Grammar: «The…the…» construction

Формула: The + comparative + …, the + comparative + …

  • The more you smoke, the worse you feel.
  • The less sleep you get, the harder it is to study.
  • The earlier you stop, the easier the recovery.

📰 Reading: «Hidden Cost of Bad Habits»

An addiction is when you cannot stop doing something — even when you know it's bad. Smoking is one of the worst: cigarettes contain over 4,000 chemicals.

The more you smoke, the higher your risk of cancer and heart disease.

How to refuse? Simple phrases: «No, thanks», «I'm not into it», «I have other plans».

Recovery is hard but possible. The earlier you ask for help, the better.
