EAT SMART

5 portions of fruits & vegetables daily. Whole grains, lean protein, healthy fats.

Eat the rainbow!
💧
DRINK WATER

At least 2 litres a day. Skip the soda — water is the best fuel for your brain.

Hydrate to feel great!
🏃
MOVE MORE

30 minutes a day. Walk, run, swim, dance — choose what you love.

Active body = active mind!
😴
SLEEP TIGHT

Teens need 8–9 hours. No phones 30 min before bed!

Good sleep = good marks!
🚫
SAY NO

No to smoking. No to alcohol. No to too much screen time.

Your choice matters!
💚
STAY POSITIVE

Connect with friends. Talk to family. Find a hobby you love.

Mental health matters!
🎯
START SMALL

You don't need to change everything today. Pick ONE habit and stick with it for 21 days.

Small steps, big results!

💡 Did you know?

A 15-year-old who develops healthy habits today is 3 times more likely to live a long, happy life. Your future self will thank you!
